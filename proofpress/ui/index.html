Keywords

  
The writer only ever picks topics from this list, highest priority first. Rows that share a cluster become one article, not several competing pages.

  

    
CSV format

    
keyword,cluster,priority,intent_hint,status,slug,published_at,notes
best plant watering app,apps,1,buying guide,todo,,,"secondaries: plant care app"
plant watering schedule template,schedules,1,list (download),todo,,,
how often to water succulents,care,2,how-to,todo,,,

    
    
      
  • keyword: required, the exact search phrase. Header row required; only "keyword" is mandatory.
    • 
      
  • priority: 1 (highest) to 3. intent_hint: buying guide, comparison, how-to, definition, troubleshooting, list, list (download).
    • 
      
  • status: todo, drafting, approved, published, blocked, skip. Leave slug and published_at empty; the pipeline fills them.
    •

Quality gate

  
Every draft is scored on a 100-point rubric by a reviewer that didn't write it. Hard fails (invented product claims, unverified YMYL claims, made-up stats, wrong intent, scanner failures) fail the draft regardless of score.
